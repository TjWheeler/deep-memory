# EuroTech Summit 2024 — Berlin

The EuroTech Summit 2024 was held in Berlin, Germany on 15-17 October 2024.

Alice Johnson gave a talk on event-driven microservices. Yuki Tanaka attended as well and presented a workshop on observability.

Robert Chen participated as a panelist in the CTO roundtable session.

Marcus Weber, founder of Nexus Technologies, delivered the opening keynote on the future of European SaaS.
