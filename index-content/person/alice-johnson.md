# Alice Johnson

Alice Johnson is a senior software engineer at Nexus Technologies. She joined Nexus in March 2021 after spending four years at Orion Systems as a backend developer.

Alice studied Computer Science at Imperial College London, graduating in 2016 with a first-class honours degree.

She currently lives in Berlin, Germany, having relocated from London, UK in early 2022.

Alice knows Robert Chen through their time together at Orion Systems, where they worked on the same infrastructure team from 2017 to 2020.
