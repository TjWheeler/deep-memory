# Priya Patel

Priya Patel is a data scientist at HealthBridge Analytics in London, UK. She joined HealthBridge in September 2019.

Priya completed her PhD in Biostatistics at King's College London in 2018. Before HealthBridge, she worked as a research associate at King's College London from 2018 to 2019.

She is married to Robert Chen. They have lived in Richmond, London, UK since 2021.

Priya volunteers as a mentor at a London-based non-profit that runs free coding courses for women.
